PRD · Build With AI: Basics

Who Hacked the Bot?

A multiplayer social deduction web game for Asian Gen Z friend groups. Players at a cartoon school rely on Study-Bot, an AI study assistant, while one secret classmate, the Bluffer, makes it lie.

status: approved

What the scope sketch became

"Friends join a game room from their own phones"→Main Menu → Create or Join by short code or auto-join link → lobby → host starts at 4+
"get secret roles"→Seated in class, open a book: your character, your role, quick facts typed out with sound
"trusts it or double-checks"→Timed multiple choice; Study-Bot recommends one; Double-check costs time and shows a hint
"Fact-Checker can verify an answer"→Once per game, TRUE/FALSE only
"2D, Among Us–style art"→2.5D cartoon school: 3D models, fixed follow camera like Mobile Legends / Super Sus; cute, chunky characters
"Anyone can call a meeting"→Button in the classroom; text chat; vote or skip; no game timer

Screen by screen

Main Menu & Lobby steps 1–4

Open the link in a phone browser. Set up your avatar on the home screen (rotating 3D preview: 4–6 characters, a color, your name; remembered on this device). Then create a lobby, or join a friend by typing the code or tapping the share link.

Acceptance criteria

  • Home shows my avatar rotating; I can change character, color, name
  • My avatar is remembered next time on this device
  • Menu shows Create Lobby and Join Friend
  • Create shows a short code and a copyable share link
  • Valid code + name adds the player to every screen's list
  • Share link asks only for a name, then joins automatically
  • Start is host-only and enabled at 4+, otherwise says how many more

What if

  • Wrong code: "room not found," try again
  • Too few players: Start disabled, "need X more"
  • Game already started: told it's in progress
Role Reveal step 5

Everyone is seated in the classroom. Each player opens a book: their character's picture, their secret role, and quick facts that type out with a typing sound.

Acceptance criteria

  • Exactly 1 Bluffer, exactly 1 Fact-Checker, the rest Students
  • Each player sees only their own role
  • Book shows character picture, role name, typed quick facts with sound

Quick facts (editable)

StudentAnswer questions at Study-Bot terminals. But careful: someone may have hacked it. Trust it, or double-check?
BlufferYou hacked Study-Bot. Make terminals give convincing wrong answers, and blend in so nobody suspects you.
Fact-CheckerYou can see the truth, once. Use it to expose a lie, but stay hidden, because if the Bluffer knows who you are, you're a target.
School Map step 6

A 2.5D cartoon school: 3D models, a fixed camera angle that follows you (like Mobile Legends / Super Sus). Move with an on-screen joystick and visit terminals from your task list.

Acceptance criteria

  • Joystick moves your character; others move in real time
  • Starts in the classroom; a few rooms to explore
  • Task list shows terminals still to visit
  • Strikes (e.g. 1/3) and class progress always visible to all

What if

  • Voted-out player: can't move, answer, vote, or chat
  • Disconnect: see assumptions
Study-Bot Terminal ★ the kernel steps 7–10

A timed multiple-choice question. Study-Bot confidently recommends one option. Trust it, or spend time to double-check.

Everyone

  • Action button near a terminal opens the question
  • Multiple choice with visible countdown
  • Study-Bot recommends one option, confident chat-bubble style
  • Double-check costs time and shows a hint
  • Correct → progress; trusted lie → +1 strike for all to see; timeout = wrong

Bluffer only

  • Hidden Hack action at terminals
  • Hacked terminal's next answer recommends the plausible lie
  • Hack has a cooldown
  • Can open terminals to look busy

Fact-Checker only

  • Verify shows TRUE / FALSE about Study-Bot's pick
  • Once per game, then it disappears
Meeting & Vote Result steps 11–12

Press the button in the classroom. Everyone is pulled into a meeting, argues in text chat, and votes someone out or skips.

Acceptance criteria

  • Any living player can press the classroom button
  • All players go to the Meeting screen
  • Text chat, only during meetings
  • Vote one player or skip; result shown to all
  • Result says whether the voted-out player was the Bluffer

Rules

  • Anyone can claim any role; the game never confirms it until Game Over
  • Play resumes after a meeting if the Bluffer wasn't caught
Game Over step 13

Class wins

  • Bluffer voted out: "kicked out of school" ★
  • Class progress reaches the goal

Bluffer wins

  • 3 strikes
  • Only the Bluffer and one other player remain
  • Shows the winning side and reveals every role
  • Way back to the menu
  • No game timer

Accepted assumptions

Placeholders I drafted, which you accepted for now. They can be tuned during the build.

  • Rooms: 4 (Classroom with meeting button, Library, Science Lab, Canteen); terminals in all but the Classroom
  • Players: 4–5, max 5
  • Tasks: 3 per player; class goal tuned for a ~10-minute game
  • Timing: ~20s per question; Double-check costs ~8s; hack cooldown ~30s
  • Meetings: 1 per player per game; ~60–90s for chat and vote
  • Fact-Checker sees TRUE/FALSE only
  • Voted-out players become spectators; disconnects are treated the same
  • Characters: now chosen in Avatar Setup on the home screen
  • Chat only during meetings
